For business owners

Industrial
and warehouse facilities

ONEZA Construction builds workshops, class A and B warehouses and logistics centres in Georgia from 1,000 m² and up to 14 m clear height in 6–12 months. 2026 guide: monolithic frame from $140 / m², ready for fit-out with utilities from $300 / m². The duration of every stage is fixed in the contract.

Commissioning goes in phases: the first phase runs while the second is being built. ONEZA plans from the production flow and calculates floors and slabs for the actual equipment and vehicles. The building is handed over ready for equipment — with floors, ramps, power supply, ventilation and treatment facilities. Payment: 100% of the materials and 15% of the work up front, then against the acts of accepted stages.

Questions

In short
the essentials

How much does a warehouse cost per m²?

The 2026 guide: monolithic frame from $140 / m², ready for fit-out with utilities from $300. Height, spans, floor loading and the utilities package drive the cost; the exact figure comes after the working design.

How long does a warehouse or workshop take?

From 6 to 12 months. Every stage has its date in the contract, and the building is handed over ready for equipment: floors, ramps, power, ventilation and treatment systems.

Can the first phase open while the second is built?

Yes, phased commissioning is the normal mode for production and warehouses. The first phase runs and earns while the second is being built next to it.

Who arranges the utility connections?

We handle the connections ourselves: power, water, ventilation and treatment are part of the design, not a separate invoice at the end. We also help with commissioning.
